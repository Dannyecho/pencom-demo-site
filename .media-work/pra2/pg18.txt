Nigeria;

(b) fix the terms and conditions of service and remuneration of the employees
of the Commission;

(c) request or call for information from any employer or Pension Fund
Administrator or Pension Fund Custodian or any other person or

(d) charge and collect such fees, levies or penalties, as may be necessary for
